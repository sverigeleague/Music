# Product ideas, 2026-10-26 (overnight pass)

Who the site serves (ROADMAP.md, DECISIONS.md): **artists and managers** ("what is my music worth, what would I keep
under each deal"), **labels and A&R** ("who is rising, who is unsigned, export"), **investors, catalog buyers and
analysts** ("who owns the market, catalog values, label financials"), and **press**.

How this list was made: ROADMAP.md (all phases), the newest decisions, the route list in `site/app.js`, and the live
local site read at 1280 and 390 px wide as each user (start page, artist page, Scout, Money, Labels, hip-hop and
country). Effort: S under a day, M one to three days, L more. Value 1-5 is our judgement of how much a paying user would
miss it. "Have it?" says whether the data is already in `site/data/index.json` / `extra.json` or the repository.

## What the users would notice today

- **Investors:** the worth figures exist per artist (`worth`, `wmult`, `age_profile`) and as a top-100 table, but there
  is no way to *screen* for deals (size, age of the catalog, label status, royalties) or to take a shortlist away.
- **A&R and investors:** an artist page is long (15+ panels). There is nothing to print or attach to an investment
  memo or a signing note.
- **Everyone, trust:** the coverage table sits at the end of Method; review queues, corrections and data dates are
  only in the repository. Competitors do not publish these; ROADMAP's "honesty features as a selling point" has no page.
- **Press:** share cards exist; there is no embeddable widget and no press page with quotable, dated numbers.

## Money: what is free, what someone would pay for

- **Free, to drive traffic and links:** the top lists, artist pages, the one-pager, the worth and pay-gap calculators,
  the data quality page, share cards and an embed widget (#8). Press quoting a dated, sourced number is the cheapest
  marketing there is.
- **What a small label pays for ($29-79/month, ROADMAP 5.5):** Scout with exports, the watchlist digest by email,
  unsigned prospects with public contact routes, saved screens. The job is "save me a day of digging a week".
- **What an investor or catalog buyer pays for (more, per seat):** the catalog screener with full exports, catalog age
  curves per artist (#6), label roster values (#7), history of market share (#15) and an API (#20). The job is
  "a defensible first screen before paying for a data room".
- **What would make it the trusted reference:** the data quality page (coverage, queues, data dates), a public
  corrections log, method versions, and later the published Scout hit rate. These cost little and are what
  competitors do not do.
- **Gates before any of it is charged for:** a licence for showing Spotify-derived figures publicly (ROADMAP 2.1);
  accounts and privacy terms (10.5); E&O insurance (Phase 6). Exports of estimates should always carry the grade and
  the "indicative, not a valuation" line.
